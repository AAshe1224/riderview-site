Chapter Setup Guide

Get your chapter set up and running in about fifteen minutes.

Wingman is a private, invite-only ride coordination app built for Greek life. Your chapter's own volunteer drivers get members home safely at night — no strangers, no surge pricing, just your people looking out for each other.

01 Create Your Circle

In Wingman, your chapter is called a Circle. After you sign up and log in, you'll be asked to create one or join an existing one — as the person setting this up for your chapter, you'll create a new Circle and become its first admin.

You'll be asked to choose a Circle type:

  • Full Circle — your members can both request rides and drive. This is the standard setup almost every chapter should use.
  • Rider Circle — your members can only request rides; there are no drivers of your own. This exists for smaller organizations that arrange to use another chapter's driver pool instead of running their own.

Heads up: whoever creates the Circle becomes its admin and gets real control over who can join and who can request rides. Make sure it's you or someone you trust with that responsibility.

02 Invite Your Members

Once your Circle exists, you'll get a 6-digit invite code. Share that code with your chapter (GroupMe, email, a QR code from inside the app — whatever's easiest) and have everyone download Wingman and enter it to join.

New members land in a pending state until you approve them — nobody can request or give a ride until an admin lets them in. This is what keeps the app invite-only and limited to your actual chapter.

03 Approve Members & Assign Roles

From Manage Users, you'll see everyone waiting for approval. For each person, you decide:

  • Approve them — they become a regular member who can request rides.
  • Also mark them as an Approved Driver if they'll be driving — this unlocks the driver tools and lets them claim shifts.
  • Also mark them as an Admin if they should have the same management powers you have (e.g., your risk manager, your social chairs who run the schedule).

You can change any of these later — approving someone isn't permanent, and neither is any role you assign.

Your priority order is yours. Roles decide who gets picked up first in the queue — that's your chapter's call entirely. Wingman never lets anyone pay to move up it.

04 Set Up Your Drivers

Once you have a few approved drivers, build out your schedule from the Schedule tab:

  • Create shifts for the nights you need coverage (e.g., Thursday/Friday/Saturday, 10pm–2am).
  • Either assign specific drivers directly, or leave a shift open for drivers to claim themselves on a first-come basis.
  • Drivers see their claimed shifts and open shifts right from their own app — no separate sign-up sheet needed.

Rollout tip: get your drivers signed up and comfortable with the app a few days before you announce it to the whole chapter. A smooth first weekend does more for adoption than any announcement.

05 How a Ride Actually Works

Worth knowing before members start asking you questions:

  • A member opens the app, enters a pickup and drop-off, and submits a request.
  • Any available driver can accept it. The member sees who's coming and can message them in-app.
  • Every ride gets a unique PIN, shown to the rider once a driver accepts. The rider gives the driver that PIN at pickup — it's a quick way for both people to confirm they've got the right car and the right person.
  • If something feels wrong during a ride, either person can press and hold the SOS button. It alerts your group's admins immediately, with the rider's location and who the driver is — and it can never be muted by anyone.

06 Admin Tools You'll Use Day-to-Day

Manage Users

Approve new members, promote/demote admins and drivers, and handle anyone who needs to be removed. Full Circles also get a separate Blocked Riders tool to cut off a specific person's ride access without removing them from the chapter entirely.

Admin Dashboard

A live view of what's happening right now — active rides, drivers online, and anything that needs your attention.

Announcements

Push a message to your whole chapter, or just your drivers — useful for "last call at 1:30 tonight" or reminding people the app exists.
