1. Who We Are

RiderView ("we," "us," "the app") is a peer coordination platform that helps members of private organizations (Greek life chapters and similar groups, referred to in the app as "Circles") organize volunteer sober rides among their own members. RiderView is not a licensed transportation company, does not employ drivers, and does not provide transportation services itself — it is a coordination tool used by members of an organization to arrange rides among themselves.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address and password (used for authentication)
  • Username / display name
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Profile details you provide, including vehicle information if you register as a driver (make, model, year, color, seat capacity)

Location Information

  • Precise location when you request a ride, submitted as your pickup point
  • Live location while you are actively driving a ride, shared with the rider(s) you are transporting and your group's admins
  • Background location, used specifically so a driver's location keeps updating for riders and admins while the app is not in the foreground during an active ride
  • Location included in an SOS alert, if location services are available at the moment you trigger one (see Section 6)

Ride and Group Information

  • Ride requests, including pickup and destination addresses, timestamps, party size, and ride status
  • Messages sent between drivers and riders within a ride
  • Your membership, role, and admin status within any Circle or sub-group you join
  • Ride history associated with your account

Device and Usage Information

  • Push notification tokens, used to deliver ride updates, safety alerts, and group announcements even when the app is not open
  • Crash and diagnostic data, used to identify and fix bugs
  • Camera access, used only to scan QR invite codes — RiderView does not store photos or video from your camera

Information From Others

  • If a member of your Circle shares an invite code or QR code with you, the code itself may contain a reference to the Circle or role you are joining

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to: - Operate the ride coordination features of the app (matching riders and drivers, tracking ride status, showing live location during active rides) - Send push notifications for ride updates, safety alerts, shift reminders, and group announcements - Allow your Circle's admins to manage membership, review ride activity within their own Circle, and respond to safety events - Maintain the security and reliability of the app, including diagnosing crashes and abuse - Enforce our Terms of Service

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising.

4. Who Can See Your Information

Because RiderView is built around private organizational groups, some of your information is visible to other people within your own Circle — this is a core part of how the app works, not a third-party data share:

  • Your Circle's admins can see your membership status, ride history within that Circle, and any safety/security events (including SOS alerts) you're involved in.
  • Drivers you're matched with can see your pickup/destination and party size for that ride.
  • Riders you're driving can see your live location, vehicle information, and name during an active ride.
  • A Guest Admin (sub-group chair), if your organization uses this feature, can see membership and roster information for the specific sub-group they manage.

5. Third-Party Services

RiderView relies on the following third-party services to operate. Each processes a limited set of data necessary for its function:

  • Supabase — our backend database and authentication provider. Stores account, ride, and group data.
  • Firebase (Google) — used for push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and crash reporting (Crashlytics).
  • Mapbox — used for address search and geocoding when you enter a pickup or destination.
  • OpenRouteService — used for calculating driving routes.

These providers may process data outside your local jurisdiction. We only share the data each service needs to perform its specific function.

6. Safety Features (SOS)

RiderView includes an SOS feature that lets you alert your Circle's admins during an active ride. When triggered, it sends your location (if available) and ride details to your Circle's admins.

RiderView's SOS feature is not connected to 911 or any official emergency response service, and does not replace calling 911. In a genuine emergency, always contact local emergency services directly.
