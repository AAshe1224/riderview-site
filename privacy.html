01 Who We Are

Wingman ("we," "us," "the app") is a peer coordination platform that helps members of private organizations (Greek life chapters and similar groups, referred to in the app as "Circles") organize volunteer sober rides among their own members. Wingman is not a licensed transportation company, does not employ drivers, and does not provide transportation services itself — it is a coordination tool used by members of an organization to arrange rides among themselves.

Contact: austin.ashe@icloud.com

02 Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address and password (used for authentication)
  • Username / display name and, optionally, phone number
  • Profile details you provide, including vehicle information if you register as a driver (make, model, year, color, seat capacity, and license plate)
  • Your notification preferences (e.g., whether you've muted chat or trip-sharing push notifications — see Section 6 for what can never be muted)

Location Information

  • Precise location when you request a ride, submitted as your pickup point
  • Live location while you're online as a driver. Your exact location is shared with the rider(s) you're currently driving and your Circle's admins. Other members of your Circle see only an approximate position (rounded to about 1 km) so they know drivers are nearby.
  • Background location, only while you're online as a driver, so riders and admins keep seeing updates when the app isn't in the foreground. Your phone shows its location indicator whenever this is on, and it stops when you go offline.
  • Your last known location, sent to your Trusted Contacts if you tap Call 911 and have that option turned on (see Section 6)

Ride and Group Information

  • Ride requests, including pickup and destination addresses, timestamps, party size, and ride status
  • Messages sent between drivers and riders within a ride
  • Your membership, role, tier, and admin status within any Circle or sub-group you join
  • Ride history associated with your account
  • If you drive: the shifts you've claimed or offered to swap, any dates you've marked yourself unavailable (visible to your Circle's admins when assigning shifts), and a record each time you confirm you're sober and fit to drive before going online
  • "Thanks" sent to a driver after a ride, and whether a rider has marked a driver as a favorite — both are one-directional appreciation signals, never a rating or score, and never affect anyone's ability to request or give rides
  • Ride reports you submit about a ride, and reports others submit about a ride you were part of
  • Records of admin actions taken in your Circle, and any review you ask for of a restriction placed on you (see Section 7)

Payment Information (Circle Admins Only)

If you're an admin renewing your Circle's semester subscription, payment is handled entirely by Stripe, our payment processor. Wingman never receives or stores your card number — you enter it directly on a Stripe-hosted checkout page. We store only a Stripe-issued customer reference ID (so a returning admin can reuse a saved card) and your Circle's subscription expiration date. Regular members never enter payment information; billing is a Circle-level action taken by an admin, not a per-person charge.

Device and Usage Information

  • Push notification tokens, used to deliver ride updates, safety alerts, and group announcements even when the app is not open. A phone's token belongs to one account at a time and is removed when you sign out.
  • Crash and diagnostic data, used to identify and fix bugs. Crash reports are not tied to your name or account.
  • Camera access, used only to scan QR invite codes — Wingman does not store photos or video from your camera

Information From Others

  • If a member of your Circle shares an invite code or QR code with you, the code itself may contain a reference to the Circle or role you are joining
  • If another member asks you to be their Trusted Contact, we store that request. You become their Trusted Contact only if you accept (see Section 6).

03 How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

  • Operate the ride coordination features of the app (matching riders and drivers, ordering the queue, tracking ride status, showing live location during active rides)
  • Send push notifications for ride updates, safety alerts, shift reminders, and group announcements
  • Allow your Circle's admins to manage membership, review ride activity and ride reports within their own Circle, and respond to safety events
  • Keep a record of admin actions so misuse of admin powers can be traced (Section 7)
  • Generate automated summaries for admins from your Circle's own existing ride and shift data (for example, a nightly recap of rides completed or a flag when a claimed shift has no driver online) — these are computed from data already described above, not a new category of information collected about you
  • Process your Circle's semester subscription payment through Stripe, if you're an admin renewing access
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the app, including diagnosing crashes and abuse
  • Enforce our Terms of Service

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising.

04 Who Can See Your Information

Wingman shows each person only what they need, and these limits are enforced by our servers, not just by the app's screens:

  • Other members of your Circle can see your name — for example when adding a Trusted Contact or requesting a ride for someone. They cannot see your phone number, vehicle, or other profile details.
  • The driver of your ride, and the rider(s) you drive, can see each other's name, phone number, tier, and the driver's vehicle (including license plate) during the ride and for 24 hours after it ends, so you can reach each other about the ride or a lost item. Riders also see their own driver's exact live location during the ride.
  • Your Circle's drivers can see a waiting ride's pickup, destination, party size, and rider name while it is waiting for a driver. Other members only see their own place in line, never anyone else's ride details.
  • Your Circle's admins can see your profile and membership details, your ride history within that Circle, ride reports, and safety and security events in their Circle (such as a driver skipping PIN verification).
  • A connected Circle's admins. If your Circle connects to another Circle's driver pool, that Circle's admins see your first name and Circle during a ride you take through them, and only "Guest rider" afterward. They cannot see your profile.
  • A Guest Admin (sub-group chair), if your organization uses this feature, can see the roster of the specific sub-group they manage.
  • Your Trusted Contacts — only people who accepted your request — receive a Trip Share link when your ride is accepted, and your last known location if you tap Call 911 with that option on.
  • Members who are pending approval or whose guest pass has expired cannot see anything about the Circle.

Trip Sharing — visible outside the app

During an active ride, you can generate a Trip Share link and send it to anyone, even someone who doesn't use Wingman. Trusted Contacts who have accepted receive this link automatically the moment a ride is accepted. Anyone with the link can view it without logging in, and it expires after 6 hours. Lost item follow-up

Within 24 hours after a ride, if you report an item left behind in the driver's car, the app opens a text message addressed directly to that driver's phone number through your own device's messaging app. That message — and anything either of you send after it — is a normal text conversation between your two phones, not something Wingman stores, sees, or controls.

05 Third-Party Services

Wingman relies on the following third-party services to operate. Each processes a limited set of data necessary for its function:

  • Supabase — our backend database and authentication provider. Stores account, ride, and group data.
  • Firebase (Google) — used for push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and crash reporting (Crashlytics).
  • Mapbox — used for address search and geocoding when you enter a pickup or destination.
  • OpenRouteService — used for calculating driving routes and estimated trip time.
  • Stripe — our payment processor for Circle subscription billing. Stripe collects and stores payment card details directly under its own PCI-DSS compliance program; Wingman never receives your full card number.

These providers may process data outside your local jurisdiction. We only share the data each service needs to perform its specific function.

06 Safety Features

Call 911

The Call 911 button opens your phone's dialer to call 911. If you've turned on "Also alert them when I tap Call 911," your accepted Trusted Contacts also receive an alert with your last known location. Your Circle's admins are not notified.

Wingman does not contact 911 or any emergency service itself. The call is placed by your phone, like any other call. In a genuine emergency, always make sure you are connected to emergency services.

Call 911 alerts to Trusted Contacts can never be muted by any notification setting in the app.

Trusted Contacts

You can ask members of your Circle to be your Trusted Contacts. They must accept before they receive anything. A Trusted Contact can decline or remove themselves at any time, and doing so is private — the person who asked is not told.

Driver fitness, ride reports, and restrictions

  • Drivers confirm they are sober and fit to drive every time they go online, and the app checks in with a driver after several hours online.
  • A rider or driver can report a problem with a ride. Reports go to the Circle's admins; the person reported is not told who reported them.
  • Only an admin can restrict a rider (probation, block, or suspension), and must give a reason, which the rider sees. A restricted rider can ask the admins for a review. Reports and ratings never restrict anyone automatically.
  • If a driver skips the rider's PIN check at pickup, the Circle's admins can be alerted and the event is kept in the Circle's security log.

07 Admin Accountability

When a Circle's admin uses their powers over another person or Circle — approving or removing a member, blocking or suspending a rider, putting someone on probation, changing someone's role, tier, or priority, granting or removing admin, guest-admin, or driver access, or changing a connected Circle's access or priority — Wingman records who did it, who or which Circle was affected, what changed, when, and any reason given. A rider's request for a review of a restriction is recorded the same way.

These records are visible only to that Circle's admins. No one, including admins, can edit or delete them. If a Circle is deleted, its records are kept for one year for resolving disputes, then permanently erased.

08 Data Retention

  • Ride details: 14 days after a ride ends, its pickup and destination addresses, map coordinates, messages, and PINs are permanently erased. A ride connected to an open ride report, or to a Call 911 alert from the last 120 days, is kept until that matter is resolved or that period ends. A ride left open for 14 days is closed automatically.
  • Trip Share links expire after 6 hours.
  • Admin action records are kept for as long as the Circle exists, and for one year after a Circle is deleted (Section 7).

When you delete your account, we immediately revoke your login access and remove your active driver and group membership records. Your ride history is retained after account deletion, since it forms part of the ride record for other members involved in those rides (drivers, co-riders) and your Circle's own safety records.

If your account is linked to a safety record that must be kept (such as a past SOS alert), certain account fields (name, phone number, vehicle details) may be retained in an anonymized or restricted form rather than fully deleted, so that the record remains intact and reviewable. Your login access is still fully and immediately revoked in this case — you will not be able to sign back in.

If you have questions about your specific data, contact us at austin.ashe@icloud.com.

09 Your Choices

  • You can leave any Circle at any time from the Account screen.
  • You can permanently delete your account and its associated data at any time from Account → Delete Account within the app.
  • You can mute non-safety push notifications (chat messages, trip-share alerts) from Account → Notification Preferences. Safety-critical alerts, described in Section 6, are never affected by this setting.
  • You can add or remove Trusted Contacts, accept or decline requests to be one, remove yourself as someone's Trusted Contact, and revoke a Trip Share link, at any time from within the app.
  • If you're restricted, you can ask your Circle's admins for a review from the restriction screen.
  • You can disable location or notification permissions at any time in your device settings, though this will limit core functionality (ride matching, live tracking, and safety alerts will not work correctly without location access).

10 Children's Privacy

Wingman is intended for use by members of college and university organizations who are at least 18 years old, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.
