02 What Wingman Is

Wingman is a coordination tool that helps members of a private organization (a "Circle") arrange volunteer sober rides among themselves.

Wingman is not a transportation company, rideshare service, or common carrier. We do not own vehicles, employ drivers, or provide transportation services. Drivers using Wingman are volunteer members of your own organization, not Wingman employees or contractors. Wingman does not conduct driver background checks, vehicle inspections, or license verification — any such vetting is the responsibility of your organization, not Wingman.

Unlike a public rideshare service, Wingman is not a marketplace that matches you with strangers. Every driver and rider on a Circle is already a vetted member of that private organization — approved by your organization's own admins before they can request or give a single ride. That vetting happens at the organization level, upstream of Wingman, which is why Wingman's role is limited to coordinating rides among people your organization has already let in, not screening the general public.

03 Eligibility

Wingman is intended for use within authorized organizations that have set up a Circle and issued you an invite code. You must be at least 18 years old to use Wingman. By using the app, you represent that you meet this requirement and that you are a legitimate member of the organization whose Circle you are joining.

05 Billing and Subscriptions

Full access to Wingman's features is provided to a Circle through a semester subscription, paid once per semester by that Circle's admin. Payments are processed by Stripe, our third-party payment processor, under Stripe's own terms of service — Wingman never receives or stores your card number (see our Privacy Policy, Section 2).

  • Subscriptions are billed per Circle, not per individual member. Regular members are never charged directly.
  • Subscriptions do not auto-renew. A Circle's admin must manually renew access each semester; access ends if it isn't renewed.
  • New Circles receive a trial period when first created. An admin may also be issued a trial code that extends that period.

Refund requests must be made within 14 days of the original purchase date. Each Circle may receive at most one refund per semester, regardless of how many times a subscription is purchased within that semester — this limit exists so a repeated purchase-and-cancel pattern can't be used to extend free access.

Wingman does not guarantee driver availability, ride completion, response time to safety alerts, or the conduct of any user, driver, or rider. You use the app, and arrange and accept rides through it, at your own risk.
