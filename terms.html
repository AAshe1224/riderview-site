02 What Wingman Is

Wingman is a coordination tool that helps members of a private organization (a "Circle") arrange volunteer sober rides among themselves.

Wingman is not a transportation company, rideshare service, or common carrier. We do not own vehicles, employ drivers, or provide transportation services. Drivers using Wingman are volunteer members of your own organization, not Wingman employees or contractors. Wingman does not conduct driver background checks, vehicle inspections, or license or insurance verification — any such vetting is the responsibility of your organization, not Wingman.

Before going online, a driver confirms that they have a valid driver's license, are insured to drive their vehicle, are not under the influence of alcohol or drugs, and are alert and fit to drive. These confirmations are the driver's own statements. Wingman records when they were made but cannot verify them.

Unlike a public rideshare service, Wingman is not a marketplace that matches you with strangers. Every driver and rider on a Circle is already a vetted member of that private organization — approved by your organization's own admins before they can request or give a single ride. That vetting happens at the organization level, upstream of Wingman, which is why Wingman's role is limited to coordinating rides among people your organization has already let in, not screening the general public.

At sign-up you confirm that you are at least 18, and Wingman records when you did. If you give a false age, you are responsible for that misrepresentation and its consequences.

04 Your Responsibilities

When using Wingman, you agree to:

  • Provide accurate information about yourself and, if applicable, your vehicle
  • Use the app only for its intended purpose of coordinating rides within your own organization
  • Not share your account, invite codes, or Co-Rider codes with anyone outside your organization or with anyone you would not otherwise let into the vehicle
  • If you drive: only go online when you are sober and fit to drive, which you confirm each time you go online, and take a break or go offline when you are tired
  • Comply with all applicable traffic and vehicle laws while driving
  • Use the contact details you can see during a ride (such as the other person's phone number) only to coordinate that ride or return a lost item
  • Not submit false ride reports, or misuse safety features such as Call 911 alerts or Trusted Contact requests to harass anyone

When you first take on a role, you accept a short conduct agreement for it. As a rider: treat your driver with respect, be ready at your pickup spot, and go to the destination you requested. As a driver: only drive sober and fit, go straight to the rider's destination without detours or extra stops, treat every rider with respect, never make advances or ask for anything in return for a ride, and never use a ride to collect information about a rider.

05 Billing and Subscriptions

Full access to Wingman's features is provided to a Circle through a semester subscription, paid once per semester by that Circle's admin. Payments are processed by Stripe, our third-party payment processor, under Stripe's own terms of service — Wingman never receives or stores your card number (see our Privacy Policy, Section 2).

  • Subscriptions are billed per Circle, not per individual member. Regular members are never charged directly.
  • Subscriptions do not auto-renew. A Circle's admin must manually renew access each semester; access ends if it isn't renewed.
  • New Circles receive a trial period when first created. An admin may also be issued a trial code that extends that period.

Refund requests must be made within 14 days of the original purchase date. Each Circle may receive at most one refund per semester, regardless of how many times a subscription is purchased within that semester — this limit exists so a repeated purchase-and-cancel pattern can't be used to extend free access.

06 Safety Disclaimer

Wingman's Call 911 button opens your phone's dialer to call 911 and can optionally alert your accepted Trusted Contacts with your last known location. Afterward, your Circle's admins see that it was used, but not your location; this is a notice, not a request for help.

Wingman does not contact 911 or any emergency dispatch service itself, and cannot guarantee that a call connects or that a Trusted Contact sees an alert. In any situation involving immediate danger, make sure you are connected to 911 (or your local emergency number).

Wingman does not guarantee driver availability, ride completion, response time to safety alerts, or the conduct of any user, driver, or rider. You use the app, and arrange and accept rides through it, at your own risk.

07 Group Admin Responsibilities

If you are an admin or Guest Admin within a Circle, you are responsible for managing your organization's membership, roles, and any risk-management policies your organization requires — Wingman provides the coordination tools but does not set or enforce your organization's own safety policies.

Admin actions are recorded. When you use your admin powers over another person or Circle — approving or removing a member, blocking or suspending a rider, putting someone on probation, changing someone's role, tier, or priority, granting or removing admin, guest-admin, or driver access, or changing a connected Circle's access or priority — Wingman records who did it, who or which Circle was affected, what changed, when, and any reason given. These records are visible to your Circle's admins and cannot be edited or deleted by anyone, including admins. If a Circle is deleted, its records are kept for one year, then permanently erased.

You are responsible for how you use these powers. Restrictions must state a genuine reason, and riders can ask you for a review. Misusing admin powers — for example to harass, exclude, or retaliate against someone — is grounds for removing your admin access.
