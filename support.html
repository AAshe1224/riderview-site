Common Questions

How do I join a Circle?

You'll need an invite code or QR code from your organization's admin.

I can't request a ride.

Make sure you've selected a party size before tapping Request. If your Circle has no drivers of its own, you may need to switch into a connected Circle first — check Account for a "Switch to a Connected Circle" option.

How do I report a safety concern?

Use the SOS button during an active ride, or contact your organization's admin directly.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and cannot be undone.

How does my Circle's subscription work?

Your Circle's admin pays a flat fee once per semester through the app, handled securely by Stripe. Subscriptions don't auto-renew, and only an admin can manage billing — regular members are never charged.
